## The Publish Queue (best strategy)

### Launch philosophy
- **Soft-launch with 3 videos on day 1** so the channel isn't a ghost town (parents + algorithm need to see a real "show"), then settle into a **steady 2/week**. Consistency beats bursts.
- **Schedule, don't hand-publish:** in YouTube Studio, upload each video as **Private → Schedule** with a future date/time. That *is* your queue — they auto-publish on cadence while you sleep.
- **Cadence:** **every 3 days at 4:00 PM IST** (10:30 UTC) — the current launch rhythm. Started 31 Jul → 3 Aug → 6 Aug → 9 Aug → 12 Aug → 15 Aug → … Re-check Studio → Analytics → "when your viewers are online" after ~2 weeks and adjust.

### Calendar (produce → schedule in this order)
| Slot | Video | Why this order |
|------|-------|----------------|
| **Launch Day** | #1 Goodnight Little Light · #5 Sleepy Colors · #2 Count the Fireflies | 3-video seed; #5/#2 reuse the library so they're ready fastest |
| Wk1 Fri | #3 Goodnight, Little Garden | introduces the friends (Dewdrop/Milo/Poppy) |
| Wk2 Tue | #4 Breathe with Lulla | the differentiator — parents search & share this |
| Wk2 Fri | **Compilation #1** (Songs 1–5, ~20 min looped to ~30) | watch-time/RPM driver |
| Wk3+ | 2 new songs/week | keep the flywheel going |

### Playlists (create on day 1 — the autoplay growth engine)
- **🌙 Bedtime Songs** (all singles) · **😴 Sleep Music (30–60 min)** (compilations) · **🎵 All Lulla Songs**
- Put every video in ≥1 playlist so autoplay chains to the next Lulla video, not a competitor.

### Milestones
- **≥5 songs →** publish Compilation #1 (30 min). New compilation every ~5 songs.
- **≥10 songs →** launch a **24/7 sleep-music live stream** (top revenue format for kids).
- **First video proves out →** dub it (Synthesia/Kapwing) into 2–3 languages → sister channels ("Lulla — Español / हिंदी"). Biggest lever to $200/mo.

### Per-upload checklist (from UPLOAD-KIT.md)
Title (front-loaded) · description w/ chapters · tags · custom thumbnail · **Made for Kids: YES** · add to playlists · schedule.
