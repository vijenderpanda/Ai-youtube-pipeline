# Brand Bible — "Lulla's Moonlit Garden"

> The un-automatable core. Every song, image, and video must obey this doc.
> Working names — **verify YouTube @handle + do a basic trademark search before locking** (steps at bottom).

---

## 1. Positioning
**The calm, cozy, low-stimulation bedtime channel** for toddlers (ages 1–4) and their tired parents. The deliberate opposite of frantic, over-saturated, hyper-cut rhyme channels. Every video is a gentle wind-down ritual.

**One-line promise:** *"Soft songs and a little glow to help small ones settle."*

---

## 2. The character — "Lulla"

An original, cuddly **night-creature** (firefly-inspired, not a literal bug) who is a *living nightlight*.

| Attribute | Spec (keep identical every time) |
|---|---|
| Body | Perfectly round, plush/matte texture, palm-sized, no sharp edges |
| Body color | Soft periwinkle **#A9B7E8** |
| Signature | A warm, glowing **heart-shaped light on the belly**, amber-gold **#FFD79A** — soft, like a nightlight |
| Antennae | Two tiny stubby antennae, each tipped with a small soft glow dot (the firefly nod) |
| Eyes | Large, low-set, gentle dark eyes; calm/sleepy; long soft blink |
| Face | Tiny rounded smile, small rosy cheeks, kind & sleepy |
| Limbs | Very small stubby arms/legs (minimal — easier to animate & keep consistent) |
| Expression range | Sleepy, content, gently curious, comforting. **Never** scared, loud, or manic. |

**Silhouette test:** a round shape + belly glow + two glow-tipped antennae must be recognizable at tiny thumbnail size. That's the brand.

### Supporting cast (keep to 3 — consistency > variety)
- **Dewdrop** — a sleepy snail with a softly glowing spiral shell.
- **Milo** — a gentle luna-moth, pale grey with pastel wings.
- **Poppy** — a tiny ladybug who yawns a lot (comic relief, very mild).

---

## 3. The world — "The Moonlit Garden"
A cozy nighttime garden, always the same recurring places so it reads as *one show*:
- **The Moon Pond** (reflects a crescent moon)
- **The Lantern Oak** (a big friendly tree with a warm hanging lantern)
- **The Glow-Flower Beds** (flowers that gently pulse with soft light)
- Ambient: drifting firefly particles, crescent moon, soft stars, gentle fog.

**Lighting:** dim, warm, low-contrast moonlight. Cozy, safe, never harsh.

---

## 4. Palette (muted & warm — a hard rule)
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Lulla body (periwinkle) | `#A9B7E8` |
| Signature glow (amber) | `#FFD79A` / deeper `#FFC66E` |
| Night sky (deep indigo) | `#2A2A4A` / `#1E1E3A` |
| Moonlight cream | `#FDF6E3` |
| Garden green (muted) | `#8FBF9F` |
| Blush accent | `#F4B8C1` |

**Rule:** desaturated, soft, dim. **No neon, no high-saturation, no harsh contrast.** This single rule is a core visual differentiator from Cocomelon-style channels.

## 5. Typography (Google Fonts — free)

- **Titles/subtitles:** `Nunito` (rounded, friendly, legible).

---

## 6. Tone & pacing rules (the anti-slop, anti-overstimulation system)
- **Tempo:** songs at **60–78 BPM**. Calm, never frantic.
- **Editing:** average shot length **4–6 seconds**. Slow pans, gentle float, parallax. **No whip cuts, no strobing.**
- **Audio:** consistent soft volume. **No sudden loud stings or jump-scares.** Music-box / glockenspiel / warm pads / soft acoustic.
- **Repetition:** soothing and rewatch-friendly, but each song has an original melody & enough variation to never read as "mass-produced" (YouTube July-2025 rule).
- **Signature ritual:** every video ends the same way — Lulla's belly light softly dims, a whispered *"Goodnight."* This is brand recognition **and** a sleep cue parents will love.

---

## 7. Song style guide
- **Genre:** gentle lullaby / soft acoustic-pop for kids.
- **Instrumentation:** music box, glockenspiel, soft piano, warm pads, light acoustic guitar, gentle "ooh/ahh" harmonies.
- **Vocals:** warm, soft, clear lead (female or child-like); simple repetitive hooks a toddler can echo.
- **Themes:** bedtime, counting stars, calming big feelings, saying goodnight, gentle colors/animals (at slow tempo).
- **Lengths:** singles 2–4 min · compilations 30–60 min · plus a 1–3 hr sleep mix / 24-7 live stream.

---

## 8. Do / Don't
| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| Muted warm palette, dim glow | Neon, oversaturation, harsh white |
| Slow pans, long takes | Fast cuts, zoom spam, strobe |
| Original soft melodies | Raw public-domain rhyme dumps |
| Same character, same world | Random unrelated clips |
| Soft consistent volume | Loud stings, jump-scares |
| End with the goodnight ritual | Abrupt/random endings |

---

## 9. Formats & cadence
- **Weekly:** 2–3 singles + 1 compilation. Same days/times.
- **Money formats:** 30–60 min compilations (watch time), playlists (autoplay), a 24-7 sleep live stream once you have ~10 tracks.
- **Scale:** replicate winners into language channels ("Lulla — Español / हिंदी / etc.") via dubbing.
