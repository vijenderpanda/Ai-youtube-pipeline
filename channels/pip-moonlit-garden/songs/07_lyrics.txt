[Intro — soft music box, no downbeat]
Round and round the moonlit garden…

[Verse 1]
Round and round the moonlit garden,
Slow as falling snow.
Every leaf is holding still
And every light is low.

[Chorus]
Round and round, little light, little light,
Circle soft and hold me tight.
Nothing hurries, nothing ends,
Only sleepy, only friends.

[Verse 2]
Round and round the sleeping flowers,
Past the quiet pond.
One more turn and one more turn
And one more turn beyond.

[Chorus]
Round and round, little light, little light,
Circle soft and hold me tight.
Nothing hurries, nothing ends,
Only sleepy, only friends.

[Bridge — hushed, same tempo, no build]
Breathe it in… and let it go…
The garden turns so slow, so slow.

[Outro — hands back to the top, no final chord]
Round and round the moonlit garden…
