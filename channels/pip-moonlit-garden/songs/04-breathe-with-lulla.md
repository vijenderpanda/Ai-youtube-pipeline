# Song #04 — "Breathe with Lulla"

**Angle:** a gentle calm-down / big-feelings breathing song → "calm down song for toddlers," "breathing exercise for kids." **This is a real differentiator** — few AI kids channels serve emotional regulation, and parents actively search for it.
**Tempo:** ~60 BPM, very soft. **Length target:** ~2:30.
**Signature visual:** Lulla's belly-glow **brightens on "breathe in," softens on "breathe out"** (pairs perfectly with the v2 breathe effect).

## Lyrics (original)

**[Intro — very soft]**
Let's take a breath together… in… and out…

**[Verse 1]**
When you feel too big inside,
And the tears want somewhere to hide,
Hold my hand and breathe with me,
Slow and soft as we can be.

**[Chorus]**
Breathe in… (in…) breathe out… (out…)
Let the worry float about.
Lulla's here, you're not alone —
Breathe it soft, breathe it slow.

**[Verse 2]**
In like you're smelling a flower so sweet,
Out like you're cooling a treat.
Feel your heart grow calm and warm,
Safe and cozy, out of the storm.

**[Chorus]**

**[Bridge — guided]**
In… two… three… … out… two… three…

**[Outro]**
There you are. All calm now. 💛
