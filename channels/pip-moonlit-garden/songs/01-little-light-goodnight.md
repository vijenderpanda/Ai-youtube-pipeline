Hero bedtime single. Original lyrics (safe to monetize). ~2:30. Tempo ~68 BPM.

## Lyrics (original)

**[Intro — soft music box]**
*(hummed)* Mmm… mmm… goodnight…

**[Verse 1]**
The moon is up, the stars are out,
The garden's soft and still.
Little Lulla lights her glow,

**[Chorus]**
Little light, goodnight, goodnight,
Twinkle soft and low.
Close your eyes, the world is calm,
Rest now, sweet and slow.

**[Verse 2]**
Dewdrop yawns inside her shell,
Milo folds his wings.
Poppy counts the fireflies,
As the quiet sings.

**[Chorus]**
Little light, goodnight, goodnight,
Twinkle soft and low.
Close your eyes, the world is calm,
Rest now, sweet and slow.

**[Bridge — softer, slower]**
Breathe in… breathe out…
The night is warm and deep.
Lulla's glow is growing dim…
It's time for us to sleep.

**[Outro — the ritual, almost whispered]**
Goodnight, little light…
*(glow fades)* …goodnight.
