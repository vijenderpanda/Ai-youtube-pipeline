# Song #05 — "Sleepy Little Colors"

**Angle:** soft colors as sleepy garden things → "colors song for babies" search demand, kept calm for bedtime (learn colors *without* the usual hyper energy).
**Tempo:** ~66 BPM. **Length target:** ~2:30.

---

## Lyrics (original)

**[Intro]**
The garden glows in sleepy colors…

**[Verse 1]**
Blue is the night and the still, still pond,
Green is the grass we are resting on.
Yellow is Lulla's little light,
Pink is the flower saying goodnight.

**[Chorus]**
Sleepy little colors, soft and low,
Drifting where the dreamers go.
Blue and green and yellow light —
Colors of the calm goodnight.

**[Verse 2]**
Purple is the sky as the stars appear,
Silver is the moon, so gentle and near.
Warm is the glow that holds you tight,
Colors tucking you in tonight.

**[Chorus]**

**[Bridge — fading]**
Soft… colors… fading… slow…

**[Outro]**
Goodnight, little colors… goodnight.
