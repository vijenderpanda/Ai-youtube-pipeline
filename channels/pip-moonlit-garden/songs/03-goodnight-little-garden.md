# Song #03 — "Goodnight, Little Garden"

**Angle:** saying goodnight to each friend + nature → "goodnight song for babies," and it *features the supporting cast* (Dewdrop, Milo, Poppy) to build the world.
**Tempo:** ~64 BPM. **Length target:** ~2:30.

## Lyrics (original)

**[Intro]**
The garden's getting sleepy now…

**[Verse 1]**
Goodnight, little Dewdrop, curled in your shell,
Goodnight, gentle Milo, you've flown so well.
Goodnight, little Poppy, tucked up tight,
The Moonlit Garden says goodnight.

**[Chorus]**
Goodnight, goodnight, little garden friends,
The day is done and the quiet descends.
Stars are out and the moon is bright —
Everybody, sleep tight.

**[Verse 2]**
Goodnight to the pond, so still and deep,
Goodnight to the flowers, drifting to sleep.
Goodnight to the moon, up in the blue,
Goodnight, little one — yes, that means you.

**[Chorus]**

**[Bridge — hushed]**
Shhh… the garden's dreaming now…

**[Outro]**
Goodnight, little garden… goodnight.
