# Calm AI — Quality Ledger
_The apple-to-apple ratchet (PRODUCTION-PLAYBOOK §15). A new episode must be **≥ the bar on every axis and strictly better on ≥1 weak axis**, with no regression on the brand spine._

## Current bar
**None — the channel has not shipped an episode.** Ep01 sets the bar and this section gets filled in with its exact params (voice + style/speed, motion model + prompts, grade, bed level, caption spec, render command).

## Scorecard axes (calm-ai specific)
Score 1–5 per episode. The first three are the channel's spine — a regression on any of them fails the gate regardless of the others.

| Axis | What "5" looks like |
|---|---|
| **Fear named in the first 3s** | The viewer hears their own worry said out loud before the topic is even introduced |
| **De-escalation is honest** | The correction is specific and sourced; we didn't manufacture calm by omitting the real risk |
| **Calm ≠ inert** | Slow pace, but the 15s hold is intact — no early cliff (`scripts/yt_retention.py`) |
| **Sourcing** | Every claim dated in `episodes/epN.receipts.md`; on-screen numbers understated vs source |
| **Visual freshness** | 100% new shots, and the shot *types* differ from the previous ep (anti-throttle) |
| **The one action lands** | A viewer could actually do it in under two minutes — or is explicitly told to do nothing |
| **Audio** | −14 LUFS, bed sits under the VO, no ducking pumping, sentence breaks intact |
