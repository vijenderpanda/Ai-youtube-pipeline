## Candidate A — "Intezaar Ki Taal" (the rhythm IS the waiting)
Hook idea: the track-clack becomes the heartbeat of waiting. Percussive, replayable.

[Chorus]
Intezaar, intezaar ki taal pe
Chal rahi hai saansein patri ke saath re
Intezaar, intezaar ki taal pe
Tu kahin, main kahin, ek hi raat re

[Verse 1]
Sheeshe pe boondein likhein tera naam
Dhundhli si wadi, dhundhle se shaam
Jo manzar guzarte hain khidki ke paar
Har ek mein dhoondhun tera hi intezaar

[Chorus]
Intezaar, intezaar ki taal pe...

[Verse 2]
Platform ki ghadi ne waqt rok liya
Bhaap ke parde ne chehra dhak liya
Seat pe padi hai teri yaad ki shaal
Chhoo loon toh lagta hai tu hai yahin, sawaal

[Bridge]
Gaadi toh pahunchegi apne thikane
Dil ka thikana toh tu hi jaane

Har seeti mein goonje bas tu, sirf tu

[Chorus — big]
Intezaar, intezaar ki taal pe...

## Candidate B — "Thehri Hui Boond" (she is the raindrop that won't slide down)
Hook idea: one raindrop stuck on the moving window = the one memory that won't leave.
Softer, more Tum-Hi-Ho ache.

[Chorus]
Thehri hui boond hai tu sheeshe pe meri
Sab kuch guzar gaya, tu wahin thehri
Thehri hui boond hai tu, main behta safar
Tujhko liye chala jaaun jaane kidhar

[Verse 1]
Khidki ke bahar bhaagte hain ped
Andar thami hai bas teri yaad
Patri ki dhun pe dil dhadakta hai
Har jhatke pe naam tera takrata hai

[Chorus]
Thehri hui boond hai tu...

[Verse 2]
Lamp ki roshni mein hilti parchhaiyan
Khaali seat pe simti tanhaiyaan
Teri odhni ka rang abhi tak wahan
Utara nahi jo station tera aaya, meri jaan

[Bridge]
Darjeeling ki dhund mein kho jaane de
Ghadi ki suiyon ko so jaane de
Jo boond na girti, use girne na doon
Jo yaad na mitti, use mitne na doon

[Chorus — big]
Thehri hui boond hai tu...
