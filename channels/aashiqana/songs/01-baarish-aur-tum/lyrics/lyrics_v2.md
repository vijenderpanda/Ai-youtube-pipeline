# Baarish Aur Tum — v2 (modern Arijit-style ballad, hook-first)

**Target vibe:** Tum Hi Ho / Saiyaara — modern emotional Bollywood ballad. Soulful expressive
male vocal, breathy tender verses building to a SOARING chorus. Piano + lush strings + subtle beat.
The hook ("Tu hi hai, tu hi hai") is the instant-connect anchor.
