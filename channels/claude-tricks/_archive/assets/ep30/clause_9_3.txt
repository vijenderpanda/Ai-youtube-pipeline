RESIDENTIAL TENANCY AGREEMENT — CLAUSE 9.3 (EARLY SURRENDER)

9.3.1 The Tenant may end this tenancy before the Fixed Term expires by serving a written Vacancy Notice on the Landlord not less than sixty (60) days before the intended move-out date.

9.3.2 On surrender the Tenant shall pay a Reinstatement Levy equal to one and one half (1.5) months' rent, less any sum already held under Schedule C.

9.3.3 Where the Vacancy Notice gives fewer than sixty (60) days, the Reinstatement Levy rises to two (2) months' rent and rent remains payable to the end of the notice period.
