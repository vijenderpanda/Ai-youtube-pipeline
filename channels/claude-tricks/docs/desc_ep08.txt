Here's one for parents. Starting August 10, 2026, Google is expanding Gemini in Google Classroom to students of all ages — not just 18+. If your child's school has already switched it on, students can turn their own class materials into flashcards, practice quizzes, and study guides right inside Classroom. For families, that means an AI study helper could sit inside the school software your kid already uses. The good news: it's admin-controlled, so schools can turn it off for under-18s. Worth a quick email to ask your child's school whether it's on.
