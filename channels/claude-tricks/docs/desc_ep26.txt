AI writing has a tell — and it isn't the em dash. It's the register: "I hope you're doing well," "due to unforeseen circumstances," "please don't hesitate to reach out." Nobody talks like that, so the moment you paste it, everyone knows. The fix is one line you add after the first draft: "Rewrite it like I'd text a friend." Same request, same chat — a form letter becomes a message a human would actually send. AI writes formal by default because you never named a voice; name one you actually use and it copies that instead.
