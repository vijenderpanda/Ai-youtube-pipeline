## 2026-08-21 — 12-candidate ideate → score → adversarial-kill pass (for the 22 Aug 00:30 slot)
Method: 4 ideation lanes × 3 ideas → single ranker → 3 independent skeptics → craft.
**All three top-ranked ideas were KILLED.** The kills are the durable output — each
names a failure mode that would have shipped:

- **Recharge Checker** (ranked #1, 80) — ✗ **THE STING WAS ARITHMETIC ON A PRICE THAT DOES NOT
  EXIST.** Indian prepaid does not sell GB; it sells VALIDITY with a DAILY CAP, and unused daily
  data expires nightly with no rollover and no resale. "GB you paid for but never used, worth
  Rs X" is a figure Claude must invent, and the prompt as drafted instructed it to. The audience
  most likely to catch it is exactly ours. → **Rule: before any money payoff, confirm the unit
  being priced is a thing that is actually SOLD.**
- **Save Button** (ranked #2, 72) — ✗ **SELF-CANCELLING PREMISE.** The sting needed "six days of
  real ticks"; the premise needed artifact state to die on close. Both cannot be true, so either
  the video must not ship or the history is staged. Also a COMPONENT, not a thing — the
  `_artifacts` failure mode (0.29/h): nobody in a cold feed wants a save button.
- **Bill Spike Finder** (ranked #3, 71) — ✗ **THE REVEAL COULD NOT BE PROVEN, AND THE PAYOFF
  BEATS THE VIDEO TO IT.** Nearly every Indian utility bill that prints a bar chart also prints
  the unit history in digits on the same sheet, so "it read the bar heights" is unprovable
  without masking (staging). And a bar chart with one tall bar is solved pre-attentively in
  ~300ms — seconds 4–8 are dead before Claude speaks. → **Rule: if the eye solves the payoff
  faster than the voice can explain it, there is no middle of the video.**

Also killed at scoring: Chat Burn Meter (needs a rate-limit wall to appear on cue — unschedulable),
Deposit Saver / Bill Decoder / Screenshot To-Do (duplicates of stronger twins).
