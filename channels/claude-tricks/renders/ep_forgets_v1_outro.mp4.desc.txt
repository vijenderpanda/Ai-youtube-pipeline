Claude just forgot what you told it.

Your chat is a box with a fixed size — the context window. When it fills (Claude's holds ~200K tokens), the oldest messages get pushed out, and the AI genuinely can't see them anymore.

The fix — paste this BEFORE your chat gets long:

"Before this chat gets long: summarize everything worth keeping so far — decisions, facts, numbers, names — as short notes I can paste into a fresh chat and continue exactly where we left off."

Then open a fresh chat, paste the notes, and continue where you left off. Works in ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini.
