Do your research – know market rate for your role/experience (Glassdoor, Levels.fyi, peer benchmarks)
Document your impact – list concrete wins, metrics, expanded responsibilities since your last raise
Pick the right timing – after a big win, before budget cycles, or at your review — avoid stressful/low-budget periods
Request a private meeting – don't ambush your boss; say "I'd like to discuss my compensation" in advance
Lead with value, not need – frame around what you've delivered, not personal expenses
State a number – anchor with a specific ask (slightly above target) backed by your research
Practice the pitch – keep it concise: achievement → market data → ask
Be ready for "not now" – ask what it would take to get there and set a follow-up timeline
Stay calm and professional – it's a negotiation, not a confrontation
