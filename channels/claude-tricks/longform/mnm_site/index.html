Privacy Policy

MissNoMeetings does not collect, store, or sell any personal data.

Your calendar is read on your device only, to show upcoming meetings and set alarms. It never leaves your phone, and there are no accounts, analytics, or ads.

The optional "Warm me up" feature sends the selected meeting's title, time, attendee names, and notes to our server for the sole purpose of generating a prep briefing with an AI model. The request is processed transiently and the content is not stored, logged, or used for any other purpose.
