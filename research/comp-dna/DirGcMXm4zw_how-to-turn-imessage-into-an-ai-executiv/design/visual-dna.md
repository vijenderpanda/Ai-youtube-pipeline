## Archetype
Talking-head + UI screenshot/mockup demo, brand-bumper bookended (product-explainer short).

## Hook mechanic (first 3s)
Not a face-first hook — opens on an abstract icon-orbit graphic (other tool logos circling the product logo) with zero text, then hard-cuts to the product's own face-logo, THEN to host talking. Curiosity is carried by motion/graphic novelty before any claim is spoken. Host's actual hook line ("it's set up... 10x simpler") lands at t=3s, reinforced by split serif-italic/bold-sans on-screen text.

## Caption grammar
Sparse, not full-sentence burned captions. 1-3 word chip captions placed low-center, lowercase bold sans, one new word/phrase per ~1s beat (e.g. "your" -> "schedule" -> "directly" -> "iMessage"). Occasionally split into two type treatments on one screen: small sans label top + large italic-serif payoff word center ("it's" / "set up"). Functions more as rhythm/emphasis marks than transcription.

## Information density
Medium-high. A new visual asset (screen recording, chat mockup, icon montage, or card list) appears roughly every 2-4 seconds, alternating with host talking-head beats. No single beat overstays — even the "full capability list" payoff only holds ~3s before it starts animating apart.

## Reusable asset components
- **iMessage phone mockup** — realistic iPhone frame, chat bubble thread, used twice as literal product proof
- **Dark rounded task-list card** ("Week task" style) — vertical stack of short capability labels, can reskin per content
- **Floating pill-chip icon cluster** — small teal rounded rects with icon+label, drifting/scattered in bg, used for both "capabilities teaser" and "stat" beats (same asset repurposed)
- **Icon-orbit constellation** — logos arranged in a circle around a center brand mark, good cold-open device
- **3D tumbling icon cluster** (Google Workspace icons) — payoff beat for "and that's it" style simplicity claims
- **Brand bumper card** — logo + wordmark centered on solid bg, used at open and close for loop closure
- **Split-caption headline treatment** — small label + large italic-serif punch word, reusable for any 2-part claim

## Colors -> content mapping notes
Mint/pale-teal (#CFE3DC-ish) = default "explainer" background for all graphic beats. Near-black (#101820-ish) = "proof/serious" beats (signup screen, dark task-list card) — darker bg signals "this is the real interface/data," lighter bg signals "this is a concept/teaser." Deep teal/green (#2F7A63-ish) = accent used consistently for pill-chips, buttons, and the product's own logo tone — keeps brand color legible against both light and dark backgrounds.

## Weaknesses / what NOT to copy
- Host framing is static and slightly underexposed relative to warm bookshelf bg — face-cam isn't the strongest visual element, it's a placeholder between graphic beats, not a draw on its own.
- Caption chips are so terse ("your", "way,", "say,") they're borderline meaningless out of audio context — don't rely on captions alone to carry the sentence for silent/muted viewers.
- Two different "phone chat" UI styles used (plain text bubbles at t=9-13 vs full iPhone-frame mockup at t=14-16, 29) — slight inconsistency in asset fidelity within the same video.
- Pacing backloads the actual payoff (full task list) to t=33s of a 41s short — risky if hook doesn't hold attention that long; wouldn't copy the slow build without a stronger 0-3s hook claim.
