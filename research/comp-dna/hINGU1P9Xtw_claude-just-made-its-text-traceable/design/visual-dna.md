## Archetype
Talking-head explainer + article/doc screenshot cutaways ("face-cam + doc proof-insert")

## Hook mechanic (first 3s)
White title card with black bold sentence-case headline states the entire news claim before any host footage. No animation, no zoom — pure text-card cut straight to talking head. Hook is informational not curiosity-gap: "Claude Now Leaves Invisible Fingerprints" tells you exactly what you're about to learn, banking on topic (Claude/AI) interest rather than a mystery hook.

## Caption grammar
Burned-in captions, one short clause at a time, changing roughly every 1-1.5s in sync with speech, bold sans, no background pill/box — just implied white text (not fully visible/confirmed at this frame rate but consistent with format). Captions are placed low-center, well clear of platform UI at the very bottom. No word-by-word karaoke highlight visible in this contact sheet; appears to be phrase-chunked static captions per line.

## Information density
Medium-low. Roughly 100s total, only 3 screenshot cutaways (~10s each) breaking up ~70s of continuous talking head. Density comes from spoken content (dense, technical) not visual density — visuals stay simple/static so viewer attention stays on the explanation.

## Reusable asset components
- "title card" — full-bleed color-block card, bold black sentence-case headline, used only at t=0 as the hook
- "doc/article screenshot insert" — full-screen capture of a source webpage/support article, with a red highlight box drawn over the specific sentence being quoted; used 3x as proof beats
- "talking-head base shot" — static medium shot, seated, plain wall bg, no b-roll cuts, host in dark tee with visible small logo/wordmark on chest
- "burned-in phrase caption" — bottom-third bold caption strip, changes per phrase

## Colors → content mapping notes
- Host segment: cool muted green-grey wall (#2E322C-ish), neutral warm skin tones, black tee — low-saturation, keeps focus on face/speech
- Proof/doc segments: white/cream background (#F2EFE9-ish) article screenshots with red (#D64545-ish) highlight boxes — the ONLY saturated color in the whole video, used exclusively to mark "this is the exact quote/evidence" — strong signal-color discipline (red = cite/proof only)

## Weaknesses / what NOT to copy
- Zero camera movement or zoom-punch for 100s straight — relies entirely on strong spoken content to hold attention; risky for less-compelling scripts
- No lower-thirds/name card identifying speaker or source publication on the doc screenshots — credibility relies on viewer trusting the screenshot is real
- No explicit CTA/end card — ends on a thesis line with no visual pointer to subscribe or next video; fine for an established channel, weak for growth
- Screenshot inserts are dense article text at full readable size but shown for only ~8-10s — likely not fully readable by the viewer, functions more as "proof exists" than "proof read"
