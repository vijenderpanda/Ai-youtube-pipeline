## Archetype
Single-take talking-head "hot take" — no b-roll, no cutaways, no title card, no UI screenshots. Pure face-cam monologue carried entirely by the script + karaoke captions.

## Hook mechanic (first 3s)
Cold open mid-sentence ("PART OF THE CHALLENGE IN 2026 IS TO ASK...") — no intro, no channel bumper, no "hey guys." Direct eye contact, already mid-energy. The hook is 100% verbal/curiosity-gap ("don't tell AI what to do — do this instead" per title), not visual. Visual hook = zero production, which itself reads as authenticity/authority signal in this niche.

## Caption grammar
- ALL CAPS, bold heavy grotesque, white fill + thick black stroke, 2 lines max, centered bottom-third.
- Word-by-word reveal timed to speech (karaoke-style), roughly 1 word per contact-sheet frame.
- One keyword per phrase highlighted in yellow (#F5E028) — always a content-bearing word (THE, AI, PICK, LOOK, FABLE, FREEHAND, BUSINESS, OBLIGATED, DEFINITION, AUTOMATION), never filler.
- No emoji, no punctuation flourishes, no animated pop/scale — flat cut-in reveal per contact sheet (can't confirm sub-second animation without raw frame check, but no dramatic scale/rotate visible across samples).

## Information density
Very low visual density, high verbal density. One idea per ~10s beat (setup → tool-drop proof → mechanism/payoff). No stacked overlays, no lower-third graphics, no counters/numbers on screen. All information carried by speech + caption text only.

## Reusable asset components
1. **Karaoke caption block** — bottom-third, 2-line max, word-level highlight-on-emphasis. Directly portable to any host-cam channel (matches existing KaraokeLine caption work in this repo).
2. **Cold-open mid-sentence hook** — a content/editing pattern, not a visual asset: trim the first 1-2s so the video starts already mid-thought.
That's it — no numbered-step card, no app-window frame, no quote slab, no before/after split, no progress pill. This reference has essentially ONE reusable visual asset (the caption grammar); everything else is bare talking-head.

## Colors → content mapping notes
- Yellow highlight = the single load-bearing keyword per caption line (attention anchor for skimming/no-sound viewers).
- Background (cream wall) and jersey color are incidental to the host's real room/wardrobe, not art-directed — do not treat as brand palette, they're themeable/irrelevant.
- No accent2 usage seen in captions; #4FA8D8 estimate comes only from the jersey stripe, not a deliberate design token.

## Weaknesses / what NOT to copy
- Zero motion/edit craft — no cuts, no zoom punches, no b-roll — relies entirely on strong verbal hook + creator authority/trust; this will NOT work for a synthetic/AI host without an established audience relationship.
- No on-screen proof of the claim (no screen recording of Codex/Fable/Freeform actually being used) — pure talk, which is a credibility gap for a newer or unverified channel.
- No explicit CTA overlay/pinned comment visual — relies on algorithm/description, riskier for franchise-style CTA needs.
- Static single angle for 35s risks retention drop after ~8-10s without any visual variation — only survives because caption pacing + keyword highlight sustains attention.
