## Archetype
Pure talking-head monologue, single unbroken shot, zero edits/b-roll/UI screenshots. "Iphone-selfie-cam news-take."

## Hook mechanic (first 3s)
No title card, no zoom punch, no cut. Opens mid-sentence energy ("We have NOW crossed the frontier...") — drops viewer into an already-moving argument. All hook weight carried by the spoken claim + bold keyword captions, not by any visual trick.

## Caption grammar
Auto-caption style, 2 lines max, uppercase, white fill/black stroke, centered bottom-third. One keyword per phrase gets yellow (#F5E642) highlight — always the "stakes" or "you" word (NOW, FRONTIER, MODELS, BEING, CYBER, WE'RE, FROM, YOU, ABOUT, REALITY, OPEN-, JUST, COUPLE, WOULD, INTO, 2026, THESE, MODELS, EVERYWHERE, INTERNET, BE, THREATS, USED, CYBER, BY, ACTORS). Roughly one highlighted word per caption chunk (~1s cadence), not whole-line emphasis.

## Information density
Very low visual density, high verbal density. One idea/sentence-fragment on screen per second; no charts, no stats overlays, no logos beyond a small jersey crest (incidental, not branding). All information delivery is spoken + captioned, nothing else competes for attention.

## Reusable asset components
- "keyword-highlight caption bar" — 2-line auto-caption block with single-word accent-color highlight, themeable per channel accent color
- that's effectively the only distinct asset; no cards, no app-window frames, no split-screens, no progress pills, no quote slabs present in this reference

## Colors → content mapping notes
- Neutral warm-gray/beige room background (#3A3F42-ish), unbranded — keeps focus on face
- Yellow (#F5E642) reserved exclusively for the one "danger/stakes" keyword per caption — trains the eye to scan for the scary word
- No secondary accent actually used in this video; accent2 above is a placeholder guess since only one highlight color appears

## Weaknesses / what NOT to copy
- Zero visual variety for 29s — works only because the speaker has strong on-camera presence/expressiveness (eyebrows, hand gesture at ~15-16s) and the topic is inherently anxiety-triggering; a flatter delivery or duller topic would not sustain this
- No proof/evidence shown on screen (no article, no screenshot, no data) — pure assertion + self-citation ("I said this months ago"), which is a credibility risk if repeated too often
- No explicit CTA/outro card — relies entirely on the closing line's shock value; doesn't reinforce channel/series branding
- Incidental wardrobe branding (football jersey) is distracting/irrelevant, not a deliberate design choice — don't replicate
