## Archetype
Talking-head prop-explainer — host holds a real printed physical artifact (a research paper on an easel) as the visual anchor, interleaved with insert cuts (paper close-ups, diagrams, brand logos).

## Hook mechanic (first 3s)
Host holds up an unlabeled printed document to camera and says "We bought this" — curiosity gap via an ordinary physical object treated as mysterious/valuable. No text-on-screen hook headline, the prop itself IS the hook.

## Caption grammar
Single running yellow caption, sentence-case, 2-4 words per screen, tightly synced to speech (near word-level pacing but not full karaoke word-highlight). No stroke/box behind text beyond a thin black outline. Caption placement floats — moves per shot to avoid host's face/held object, no fixed safe-zone box.

## Information density
High — one new visual beat almost every 1-2s (paper insert → diagram → 3D render → logo → back to host). Script is dense, no filler; content escalates from "famous paper" → "the architecture" → "GPT stands for" → "ChatGPT wouldn't exist without it." Classic zoom-out-the-stakes structure in 33s.

## Reusable asset components
- **Held-prop board**: host holds a large printed page on an easel/whiteboard stand — reusable as "physical document reveal" component
- **Paper close-up insert**: full-bleed photographed document page, static, held ~1s
- **Abstract diagram insert**: animated node/edge diagram (neural net) — themeable data-viz component
- **3D render insert**: GPU rack / server render — stock-asset insert for "compute" beats
- **Architecture diagram insert**: labeled technical figure (Transformer arch) — swappable per topic paper
- **Brand logo stamp insert**: centered app icon (ChatGPT) on plain bg — "made possible by X" component
- **Running caption bar**: yellow sentence-case caption, dynamic position, no chip/background

## Colors → content mapping notes
- Neutral off-white/beige office bg (unstyled, authentic — NOT a branded set) keeps focus on host+prop
- Yellow (#F5D400-ish) caption = high-contrast accent against muted room, consistent brand thread through the whole video
- Inserts (diagrams, logos) are black/white/grayscale — contrast pop against the yellow caption, keeps palette from feeling busy

## Weaknesses / what NOT to copy
- Zero on-screen hook text in first 3s — relies entirely on verbal delivery + prop; risky for AI-avatar remake since prop can't be "held up" as naturally
- Raw/unpolished background (visible office clutter, wall vents) — reads as authentic but is not a designed set; if replicating, deliberately choose whether authenticity or polish is the goal
- No lower-third channel branding, no CTA card/end-screen visible in these 33s — leaves discovery/subscribe unaddressed
- Sentence trails off at t=32s ("extremely...") — ambiguous whether video is cut short or intentional; don't copy an accidental abrupt ending
- Single static host framing (no b-roll of host's face reacting) makes the piece rely 100% on scripting/pacing to sustain interest
