# Visual DNA — FtyePPCE5q0 "My Biggest AI Mistake" (David Ondrej)

## Archetype
Talking-head + diagram pip — host circle-masked over rotating technical diagrams (git graphs, benchmark charts, node trees, mascot illustrations), interleaved with full-frame talking-head beats.

## Hook mechanic (first 3s)
Full-frame host, hands clasped, static plain-wall bg, over a stark red-bg/white-text claim card: "Biggest AI coding mistake". No cold open b-roll, no VO before text — text card appears on frame 1. Pure claim-first hook, zero setup.

## Caption grammar
One short phrase (2-5 words) per card, word/phrase-synced hard cuts roughly every 1s (matches speech cadence, not fixed timer). Bold sans-serif, yellow fill, thick black stroke, centered ~60% down frame, sits below the host circle so it never overlaps the face. No animation-in on the caption itself — it's a hard swap, all motion energy comes from the cut rate.

## Information density
High — a new diagram/chart every 2-6s, but each diagram is simple (2-6 elements: dots, boxes, one branch line). Density comes from cut frequency, not per-frame complexity. Diagrams are throwaway/generic-looking (default draw.io/whiteboard style), not polished — signals "notes", reinforces authenticity over production value.

## Reusable asset components
- **Host circle pip** — circular-masked talking head over full-bleed background asset; bg toggles dark<->cream per beat
- **Claim card (hook)** — full-bleed color block (red) + white bold headline text, 2-line max
- **Git-graph diagram** — branching commit-dot graph (procedural, animates node-by-node as narration progresses)
- **Repo/folder tree diagram** — box-and-line hierarchy (Repository → worktree folders → branch labels)
- **Node-flow diagram card** — labeled root node branching into 2 leaf cards (e.g. "IMPLEMENT USER AUTH" → two agent output cards) on cream card bg
- **Benchmark line/scatter chart** — labeled model-comparison chart, used purely for authority/credibility, not literal explanation
- **Mascot/pixel-art icon** — small robot-at-monitor illustration personifying "the AI agent" on plain cream bg
- **Lower-third sponsor badge** — small logo pinned bottom-center during full-frame talking segments (brand mention, persistent not per-beat)

## Colors → content mapping notes
- Dark near-black bg = default/talking-head and most diagrams (git graphs, node trees)
- Cream/off-white bg = "explainer aside" moments (mascot, benchmark charts, folder trees, node-flow cards) — signals a tonal shift to "let me show you simply"
- Yellow caption text = universal constant regardless of bg color (works on both dark and cream)
- Red only appears once, in the hook card — reserved for the single highest-stakes claim

## Weaknesses / what NOT to copy
- Diagrams are generic/low-fidelity (look like screen-grabbed doodles) — don't copy the low-polish, only the pacing/structure logic
- No explicit end-card CTA visible in captured range (ends mid-explanation at t=49s of 50s) — can't confirm outro pattern, don't assume one
- Circle-pip host mask crops tightly and sometimes clips forehead/chin at edges — keep more margin if rebuilding
- Benchmark chart insert (t=10-11s) is tangential filler for authority, not directly explanatory — thin content, use sparingly
