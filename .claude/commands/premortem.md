It is six months from now and this has failed badly: $ARGUMENTS

Write the post-mortem from that future. Don't hedge — assume it went wrong.

1. **The failure** — one blunt sentence on what actually broke.
2. **The three causes** — the specific decisions or assumptions that led here, most-likely first. For each, name the moment we could have caught it.
3. **The early warning we'll ignore** — the signal that will show up soon and look harmless.
4. **The one change now** — the single highest-leverage thing to do today that most reduces this failure's odds.

Be concrete and specific to my plan, not generic risk-management advice.
