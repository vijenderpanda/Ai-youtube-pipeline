Steelman this — build the strongest, most honest case FOR it, stronger than I could make myself: $ARGUMENTS

Rules:
- Assume a smart, informed person believes this for good reasons. Find those reasons.
- No strawman, no faint praise, no "some might say." Argue it like it's your own hill.
- Use the best available evidence and the most charitable reading of the intent.
- Then — and only then — one closing line: the single strongest point that survives even after the steelman. That's the part I should actually engage with.

If, after building the best case, the position still doesn't hold, say so plainly and name the one load-bearing claim that fails.
