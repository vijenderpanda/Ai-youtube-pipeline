Before I decide this, scout the ground: $ARGUMENTS

Don't give me an answer. Give me the map of what I don't yet know.

1. **The three facts that would settle it** — the specific unknowns that, once answered, make the decision obvious. For each, how I'd find it out fast.
2. **The cheap test** — the smallest experiment that de-risks the biggest assumption before I commit real time or money.
3. **The point of no return** — the step after which this gets expensive to reverse, so I know how long I can stay flexible.
4. **The question I should be asking but am not.**

Prioritize by what unblocks the decision soonest.
